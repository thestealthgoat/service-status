Service Status

A live “where are we?” screen for your booth, iPad and phones.

  • The message: which point the pastor is on, how far through, and when he’ll finish.
  • Songs: which section the band is in, from your ProPresenter slides.
  • The order of service: what’s on now, what’s next, and how far ahead or behind you are, from Planning Center.

Read-only: it never changes anything in ProPresenter or Planning Center. Installs into Applications and opens when it’s done.
